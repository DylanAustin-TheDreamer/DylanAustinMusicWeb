The story

A softer
kind of loud.

Dylan Austin writes from the space between things: the last light of the day, a room after everyone has left, the feeling of a familiar song coming back around.

Based in London and raised on a steady diet of soul, folk, and late-night radio, his music leaves room for the listener. Each song is an invitation to slow down, breathe out, and stay a little longer.
