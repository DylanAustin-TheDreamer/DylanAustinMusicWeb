Dylan Austin is a musician and singer for hire.

Playing covers and taking requests from audiences in order to learn and play what people like to hear. Currently based around west Yorkshire, he performs at local venues and events, bringing his unique sound to the community.

If you are looking for a unique and talented musician to perform at your event, venue, function or wedding - then Dylan is not a bad choice. Well mannered, professional, and always ready to deliver a great performance.
